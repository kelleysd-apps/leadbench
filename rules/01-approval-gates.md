# Rule 01 — Approval Gates

These actions ALWAYS require explicit operator approval in chat before execution:
- Sending or scheduling any outreach (email, DM, message) to any person.
- Posting publicly (communities, social, forums).
- Writing to the app database or any system of record.
- Importing a sourced list into "reach" (Stage 2+).
- Sending to anyone marked Do Not Contact = true, or a Contact not yet cleared from
  'review-before-contact'. (These should simply never be sent to.)

When presenting for approval, show: who would be contacted, the exact message, the channel,
and the guardrail check. Be concise. Wait for an affirmative ("yes", "approved", "send it").
