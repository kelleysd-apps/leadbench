# examples/ — a complete worked instance

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — every company, person, and address below is invented. Do not contact
anyone listed here.

This folder is a fully filled instance of `active-focus/` and `brain/`, built for an entirely
fictional market so you can see what "filled" actually looks like before you fill your own.
It is not a template to copy fields out of — it's worked reference material.

## What's here

- **`active-focus/focus.md`** and **`brain/*.md`** — the same six files that live at the repo
  root, filled for the fictional market: independent bicycle repair shops that outgrew their
  paper/spreadsheet booking system.
- **`ANNOTATED.md`** — the file that actually transfers. It walks through every non-obvious
  decision in the fills above and explains *why* — why the ICP is narrowed the way it is, why
  each disqualifier exists, why the offer is phrased the way it is. Read this one first.
- **`outreach/`** — one worked message family for the fictional market, showing the compliant,
  non-spammy form the real `outreach/` folder should follow.

## How to use this

1. Read `ANNOTATED.md` first — it's the reasoning, not just the shape.
2. Read the filled files it's annotating, side by side.
3. Close this folder. Go fill your own `active-focus/focus.md` and `brain/*.md` from a blank
   `<FILL:>` prompt, in your own words, for your own market.

Do not copy sentences out of this folder into your real fill. A copied sentence describes the
bicycle-shop market, not yours — and the entire point of `brain/` is that the operator did the
thinking. Filling by imitation defeats the purpose before you've started.

## Why this market

Independent bicycle repair shops were picked deliberately: boring, non-sensitive, no
regulated advice, no vulnerable population, nothing that reads as a real identifiable company
if you squint. That's what a worked example should be — legible on its own terms, without
borrowing weight from a "serious" industry to feel credible.
