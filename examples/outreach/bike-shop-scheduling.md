## Notes for whoever reuses this format

- `{{observed_signal}}` must be something actually true and specific to this recipient — a
  review, a website line, a social post. Never invent one to have something to open with; if
  nothing verifiable exists for a lead, that lead isn't ready for this family yet.
- Every email keeps the ask to one thing, and the ask gets smaller to larger across the
  sequence (watch a video → nothing → try a trial), never the reverse.
- The opt-out line is not decorative — a STOP reply must actually suppress the contact,
  immediately, in whatever system tracks sends.
