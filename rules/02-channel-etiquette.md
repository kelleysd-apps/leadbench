# Rule 02 — Channel Etiquette & Deliverability

- Respect opt-outs and unsubscribes permanently and immediately.
- Honor CAN-SPAM / GDPR-style basics: identify who we are, give a real way to opt out,
  no deceptive subject lines.
- No purchased lists used for sending. No scraped personal data.
- Cold email: warm the domain, keep volume sane, personalize from real facts.
- Communities: contribute before pitching; follow each community's self-promo rules.
- If a channel's terms prohibit outreach automation, don't automate there.

## Cold email (default primary channel — confirm against active-focus/focus.md)
- Personalize from real facts. The concrete sourcing surfaces (which databases, registries,
  or public sources supply those facts for the current market) are an operator fill — see
  brain/vertical-profile.md. This rule doesn't hard-code any of them; it holds for whichever
  ones the current focus uses. No generic blasts.
- Identify who we are, give a real one-click opt-out, no deceptive subject lines (CAN-SPAM).
- Use only verified/public work emails (per rule 03's sourcing standard). Never guess or
  pattern-generate an address for sending.
- Keep daily volume sane; warm the sending domain; respect any unsubscribe permanently.

## LinkedIn (secondary — manual only, if the focus uses it) — account safety is existential
If the current focus includes LinkedIn as a channel, the account is **never** automated or
scraped by the bench — if it's flagged, the machine dies. So:
- **Sourcing never logs into LinkedIn.** Leads come from whichever public sources
  brain/vertical-profile.md names for this focus.
- **Any LinkedIn touch is sent by the operator, by hand.** The bench only drafts the
  connection note + message; it never connects or messages on LinkedIn itself.
- Keep it a low-volume, high-personalization human touch (the operator's own judgment on
  daily count). No bulk connecting, ever.
