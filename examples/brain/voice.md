# Brand Voice — bike-shop-scheduling-v1 (worked example)

## Tone (3-5 adjectives)

Plainspoken, respectful of the trade, unhurried, specific. We talk to a shop owner like
someone who's earned the way they run their business, not like a prospect who needs
convincing that their current system is bad.

## We sound like

> You know the feeling when the board's three-deep and you can't remember whose bike is
> whose? SpokeDesk keeps every ticket matched to the bike and the customer automatically, so
> Saturday morning stays Saturday morning instead of a scramble. Want a 2-minute video of what
> it looks like on an actual shop's board?

Leads with the specific chaos moment, names the exact fix in one sentence, asks for almost
nothing (watch a video, not "buy now").

## We never sound like

> 🚀 Revolutionize your bike shop with next-gen AI-powered scheduling technology! Don't get
> left in the dust by the competition — sign up today before spots fill up!

Wrong because: hype words, no specific pain, implies the shop is already behind (they're not
— their current system got them this far), and manufactures urgency that doesn't exist for a
$19/month self-serve tool.

## Banned phrases / clichés

"revolutionize", "game-changer", "next-gen", "AI-powered" as a value claim, "leverage",
"seamless", "don't get left behind", "in today's fast-paced world", any invented scarcity
("spots filling up", "limited time").

## Rules

- Lead with the specific chaos moment, not the product category.
- One ask per message — a reply, a video watch, a trial start — never more than one.
- No false urgency, no fake scarcity.
- Respect that their current system built a real business — never imply they're behind the
  times for using paper or a spreadsheet.
- Keep the first-touch ask small enough that "no thanks" costs the reader nothing.

## What would you never say, even if it worked?

We would never say or imply "the way you've been running your shop is wrong." Even if a
harder-edged message tested better, undermining a shop owner's confidence in a business
they've run for years isn't a trade-off this brand makes for reply rate.
